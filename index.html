Projects

iMary Closet

An iPad “digital closet” inspired by iCarly. It catalogs my whole wardrobe, previews outfits on a photo of me, and suggests looks based on the weather and occasion using a stylist that learns my taste.

In one iCarly episode, Carly’s bedroom gets rebuilt after a fire and comes with a digital closet: she browses her clothes on a screen and picks an outfit. I wanted one of my own, so I built it.

iMary Closet is a touch-friendly web app that runs on an iPad mounted near my closet. Every piece of clothing I own is cataloged with photos and tags for category, color, season, and fit, so browsing feels like flipping through hangers, not scrolling a spreadsheet.

  • MirrorShows outfits on a photo of me, so I can see how a top, bottoms, and shoes look together before I put them on.
  • Style meBuilds outfits from clothes I already own, using the live Pittsburgh forecast plus the occasion, time of day, and season.
  • A stylist that learnsI rate outfits (“Not me,” “I’d wear it,” “Love it”). The recommender runs entirely on the device and adapts to how I actually dress, giving less weight to older ratings over time.
  • InsightsMost- and least-worn pieces, cost per wear, pairs that work, pieces that might be ready to go, and wardrobe gaps, including which single new piece would add the most outfits for a season.
  • Sell it kitCrops face-free listing photos, writes a title and description, suggests a price, links to Depop, eBay, Poshmark, and Vinted, and tracks what I’ve listed and sold.

The goal: make getting dressed easier, wear more of what I already have, and buy less.

Coming next: reading clothing tags from a photo, bags and accessories in outfit suggestions, trip packing lists, and quick logging from my iPhone.
